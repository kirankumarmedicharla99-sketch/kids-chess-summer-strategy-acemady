Stratagem Academy Parent Portal Governance & Youth Safeguards

1. Parent Account Responsibilities

The Stratagem Parent Portal is designed exclusively for verified parents and legal guardians registering youth students (ages 5–16) for instructional chess programs and summer camps. Parents agree to provide accurate guardian contact details for safety and emergency notifications.

2. Youth Safety & SafeSport Standards

Stratagem Academy adheres strictly to USCF and FIDE SafeSport guidelines. Student progress analytics, match logs, and camp photo galleries are restricted to registered guardians with authenticated credentials.

3. Privacy & Data Protection

We do not sell, license, or share student or family data with third-party advertisers. All guardian credentials and enrollment records are encrypted under industry best practices.
