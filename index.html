About the Academy

MORE THAN A GAME.
A MINDSET FOR LIFE.

Stratagem is an elite summer sanctuary where cognitive discipline converges with youthful curiosity. We believe that chess is the definitive laboratory for intellectual poise, spatial foresight, and resilient self-mastery.

Through mentor-led strategy circles, our campers learn to foresee consequences before committing to a move. We cultivate strategic thinking, acute concentration, decision making under pressure, and enduring sportsmanship that transcends the 64 squares.

The Camp Experience

A SUMMER BUILT AROUND STRATEGIC THINKING.

Every day blends intellectual rigor with vibrant social discovery, expert mentorship, and camaraderie.

STRATEGY LAB

Hands-on chess challenges, interactive puzzles, and tactile endgame labs designed to make strategic thinking deeply thrilling and intuitive.

COACH-LED LEARNING

Small-group mentorship capped at 6:1 ratios, ensuring personalized feedback, patience, and bespoke intellectual milestones for each child.

PLAY & COMPETE

Friendly blitz arenas, team match strategies, and encouraging mini-tournaments that build graceful sportsmanship and real confidence.
